How to Play 🎤

  1. Everyone joins the room on their own phone (2+ players).
  2. Each round, the game randomly picks a Guesser and a Scorekeeper.
  3. The Guesser puts their phone face down — everyone else sees the secret word.
  4. Shout clues! (Don't say the word or spell it!)
  5. When the Guesser gets it (or gives up), the Scorekeeper taps ✓ Got it or ✗ Skip and the next word appears.
  6. Watch for ✨golden words (double points!) and 🔥streaks (3 in a row = bonus point!).
  7. Teams mode: only the Guesser's teammates shout clues — the other team stays quiet!
  8. Escalation mode: the same 30 words come back in 3 phases — describe freely, then one-word clues, then act it out!
